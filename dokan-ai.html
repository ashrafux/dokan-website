4 Simple Steps
to Get Started

Enable it once for the whole marketplace,
then anyone can connect their assistant and start asking.

STEP 1

Switch it on

Turn the feature on for your whole marketplace, one time.

STEP 2

Set up access

Everyone generates their own secure login in WordPress. It ties the assistant to exactly what they’re allowed to reach.

STEP 3

Connect

Paste it into Claude, Cursor, or VS Code. Copy, paste, done.

STEP 4

Start asking

Say “who am I on this marketplace?” and you’re rolling.
