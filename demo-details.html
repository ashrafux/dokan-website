Vendor Storefronts with
Company Profiles

Give each vendor a customizable storefront with their own company profile. Build trust, showcase credibility, and help buyers connect faster.

Bulk Product Listings

Let vendors upload hundreds of products at once using CSV files. Save time, cut manual work, and help sellers scale faster without the tech struggles.

Request for Quotation
(RFQ) System

Let buyers send custom quote requests directly to vendors. Perfect for bulk deals, negotiations, and B2B-style purchasing, just like Alibaba.

Tiered & Wholesale Pricing

Offer different prices based on quantity. Vendors can set custom pricing tiers to encourage bulk purchases and grow their average order value.

Multiple Commission Types

Set flexible commission rates for vendors by percentage, flat fee, or a mix of both. Perfect for managing different seller types and pricing models.

Advanced Order Management

Easily track, filter, and manage orders across multiple vendors. Dokan’s powerful order system keeps everything organized for smoother fulfillment.

Built-in Shipping Zones & Rules

Vendors can set shipping rates by location, product, or zone. Manage local and international deliveries with full control over shipping options.

Real-Time Vendor Analytics

Vendors get access to sales reports, earnings, and order stats from their dashboard. Help them make smarter decisions and grow their business faster.
